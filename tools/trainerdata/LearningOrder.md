# Leveling plan per class (WoW Forever)

What to buy at the trainer while leveling 1-60, what to skip, and money tips. Generated from
`advice/<CLASS>.json` by `build_trainer_spells.py`; the per-spell marks are in `TrainerSpells.lua`.
Default viewpoint: solo leveling. Costs: see `TrainerSpells.lua` (base price, before reputation discount).

## Warrior

_All class trainer ranks 1-60: 203g 62s. Marked recommended: 77g 24s. Marked skip: 33g 20s._

**Solo default: Arms (2H) or Fury (DW); Protection is viable but slow per Warcraft Tavern Forever.**

- **1-10:** Battle Shout, Charge, Rend, Parry, Thunder Clap, Hamstring, Heroic Strike. Do the Defensive Stance quest at 10 (grants Sunder Armor, Taunt).
- **10-14:** Bloodrage, Rend 2, Battle Shout 2, Overpower, Shield Bash. Tactical Mastery at 14 (baseline in Forever).
- **14-20:** Demoralizing Shout and Revenge are optional (tank/Prot). Skip Mocking Blow. Shield Block only with a shield. Disarm is optional.
- **20:** Victory Rush and Retaliation are key. Rend 3 yes. Dual Wield only for Fury, Slam only with Improved Slam, Cleave rank 1 for groups.
- **22-28:** Battle Shout 3, Sunder Armor 2, Execute (24), Heroic Strike 4, Charge 2. Intimidating Shout optional. Skip Challenging Shout solo.
- **30:** Berserker Stance quest. Rend 4, Overpower 2 (Arms). Berserker Rage optional.
- **32-40:** Battle Shout, Heroic Strike, Execute ranks. Skip Hamstring 2 and Shield Bash 2. Whirlwind at 36. Pummel at 38 (your shield-free interrupt). Plate Mail at 40.
- **40-60:** keep Battle Shout, Heroic Strike, Execute, Rend, Sunder and Charge current. Talent ranks (Mortal Strike, Bloodthirst, Shield Slam at 48/54/60) are very cheap; buy them if talented.
- Thunder Clap, Cleave, Overpower (non-Arms) and Demoralizing Shout ranks: buy only if you use them a lot.

**Money tips**
- Weapon upgrades matter most: warriors get 95-100% of damage from the weapon (Method.gg).
- Safe skips: Mocking Blow, Challenging Shout, Hamstring ranks 2-3, Shield Bash ranks 2-3, Pummel rank 2.
- Recklessness (50) is a nice burst but not needed to level.
- Save for riding at 40; Skinning or Mining are easy early income (Method.gg).
- Tank in dungeons? Then Thunder Clap, Demoralizing Shout, Revenge, Shield Block, Shield Wall become core buys.

## Paladin

_All class trainer ranks 1-60: 252g 32s. Marked recommended: 77g 47s. Marked skip: 66g 53s._

**Solo default: Retribution (Seal of Righteousness / Seal of Command, Judgement, Holy Strike). Protection is a durable alternative.**

- **1-10:** Devotion Aura, Blessing of Might, Judgement, Holy Strike (6), Divine Protection, Holy Light, Hammer of Justice, Purify, Parry. Core loop: seal, Judgement, Holy Strike on cooldown.
- **10:** Seal of Righteousness rank 2, Blessing of Protection, Lay on Hands. Seal of Fury and Righteous Fury only for tanking.
- **12-18:** Redemption rank 1 comes from the class quest. Holy Strike, Holy Light, SoR ranks yes. Retribution Aura at 16 is your main aura. Seal of the Crusader ranks can be skipped.
- **20:** Blessing of Kings and Consecration (baseline now). Exorcism and Flash of Light are optional (Flash is core for healers).
- **22-28:** Skip Concentration Aura, Seal of Justice, Turn Undead, Shadow Resistance Aura. Hammer of Justice 2 and Holy Light 4 yes.
- **30:** Lay on Hands 2, Holy Light 5. Seal of Command rank 2 if talented (cheap). Seal of Light optional. Skip Divine Intervention.
- **34:** Divine Shield. Keep Seal of Righteousness/Holy Strike/Retribution Aura current.
- **38-44:** Seal of Wisdom optional. Plate Mail at 40. Cleanse at 42 (optional solo, core for healers). Hammer of Wrath at 44 is a good execute.
- **46-60:** Keep Holy Strike, SoR, Holy Light, Blessing of Might and Retribution Aura current. Skip resistance auras, Blessing of Sacrifice and Greater Blessings while leveling.
- Talent ranks (Seal of Command, Holy Shock, Holy Shield, Light's Vigil) are very cheap; buy them if talented.

**Money tips**
- Safe skips: all Resistance Auras, Turn Undead, Divine Intervention, Seal of Justice, Concentration Aura, Greater Blessings.
- Higher Redemption, Divine Protection and Blessing of Protection ranks mainly add duration or resurrect health.
- Devotion Aura ranks only matter if you use it instead of Retribution Aura (e.g. tanking).
- The level 40 mount is free (Method.gg); dual spec costs 50g at 40.
- Consecration drains mana quickly while soloing (Method.gg); upgrade only if you AoE or tank.

## Hunter

_All class trainer ranks 1-60: 206g 42s. Marked recommended: 87g 91s. Marked skip: 43g 21s._

**1-10:** Buy everything: Serpent Sting, Arcane Shot, Hunter's Mark, Concussive Shot, Aspect of the Monkey, Parry. Track Beasts/Humanoids are cheap.
**10:** Most important level (pet quest + Aspect of the Hawk). Train Growl, Great Stamina, Natural Armor for the pet right away.
**12-20:** Mend Pet, Wing Clip rank 1, Multi-Shot (18), new Serpent Sting/Arcane Shot/Hawk ranks. Skip Eagle Eye, Eyes of the Beast, Scare Beast, Track Undead, Distracting Shot.
Ranged specs (BM/MM) can skip Raptor Strike ranks and Mongoose Bite; Survival buys them (plus Immolation Trap, Dual Wield).
**20:** Aimed Shot (now baseline), Aspect of the Cheetah, Freezing Trap, Mend Pet 2. Skip pet resistances and Disengage unless grouping.
**22-30:** Hunter's Mark 2, Rapid Fire (26), Frost Trap (28), Feign Death (30), Aimed/Arcane/Hawk/Sting ranks. Skip Scorpid Sting (optional), Beast Lore, Track Hidden/Elementals.
**30-40:** Growl ranks for the pet, Explosive Trap optional for AoE pulls. Skip Flare, Track Demons/Giants, Viper Sting, Wing Clip 2.
Save gold for the level 40 mount; Mail at 40 is a must, Aspect of the Pack and Volley are optional.
**40-60:** Keep Aimed Shot, Serpent Sting, Hawk, Mend Pet, Hunter's Mark and Growl current. Skip Aspect of the Wild, Track Dragonkin, Freezing Trap 2/3 if short on gold.
**Talent ranks** (Summon Hawk, Trueshot Aura, Sniper Shot, Counterattack) only show up if you took the talent; buy them when they do.
Arcane Shot ranks 4+ are low priority (Method: weak after ~25; BM's Summon Hawk shares its cooldown).
Money: Skinning (+ Leatherworking or Mining) is the usual hunter money pair; buy cheap food in bulk.

## Rogue

_All class trainer ranks 1-60: 159g 72s. Marked recommended: 41g 82s. Marked skip: 29g 97s._

**1-10:** Buy everything (Classic advice: train all up to 20). Stealth, Pick Pocket, Gouge, Evasion, Sinister Strike/Eviscerate ranks. Backstab only if you use a dagger.
Do not open every mob from Stealth at low level; it slows you down (Warcraft Tavern).
**10:** Dual Wield first (huge damage boost), Slice and Dice, Sprint. Sap is optional (group CC).
**12-20:** Kick, Parry, Sinister Strike 3, Eviscerate 3. Garrote/Ambush/Backstab only for dagger or Subtlety builds. Expose Armor is optional (strong on long fights in Forever).
Skip Feint (group threat), Gouge ranks 2+ and Kick ranks 2+ (only add damage).
**20-30:** Vanish (22), Cheap Shot (26), Kidney Shot (30), Sinister Strike/Eviscerate ranks. Poisons class quest at 20.
Rupture: Subtlety bleed builds buy it, others can skip. Skip Distract, Detect Traps, Disarm Trap.
Pick Lock (16) is optional: lockboxes and some quest chests.
**30-40:** Keep Sinister Strike, Eviscerate and your spec's builder/opener current. Stealth ranks and Sprint 2 are nice but optional. Save for the 40 mount.
**40-60:** Mutilate ranks if you took the talent. Slice and Dice 2 (42), Kidney Shot 2 (50). Skip Safe Fall, Feint ranks, Sap ranks unless grouping.
Spec focus: Combat = Sinister Strike, Slice and Dice, Eviscerate; Assassination = Mutilate, Ambush, Slice and Dice; Subtlety = Ambush/Garrote/Rupture (+ Hemorrhage talent).
Money: pick pocket humanoids (opener macro), Skinning + Leatherworking; use buff food for the XP bonus.

## Shaman

_All class trainer ranks 1-60: 210g 27s. Marked recommended: 79g 36s. Marked skip: 50g 72s._

**Solo default: Enhancement** (both Forever guides call it the fastest leveling spec).

- **1-10:** Rockbiter Weapon, Earth Shock, Lightning Shield, Earthbind Totem, Healing Wave. Pull with Lightning Bolt, Earth Shock, melee. Skip Stoneclaw Totem.
- **10-20:** Flame Shock, Strength of Earth Totem, Searing Totem (quest), Fire Nova (12), Cure Poison. Purge and Tremor Totem are optional. Flametongue Weapon only if you use a shield/caster setup.
- **20:** Ghost Wolf, Call of the Elements and Totemic Recall are the key buys. Frost Shock and Lesser Healing Wave are optional; Frostbrand Weapon can be skipped.
- **22-28:** Totemic Call (projection) is optional (very mana-expensive). Skip Frost/Fire Resistance Totems and Far Sight. Buy Magma Totem and Mana Spring Totem at 26.
- **30:** Windfury Weapon (switch to a slow two-hander), Reincarnation, Astral Recall. Skip Nature Resistance Totem; Grounding Totem is PvP.
- **32-40:** Lightning Shield, Healing Wave, Earth/Flame Shock and totem ranks. Windfury Totem is a party buff only (you don't benefit while using Windfury Weapon). Skip Sentry and Windwall Totem. Mail at 40.
- **40-60:** keep shocks, Lightning Shield, Searing/Magma, Strength of Earth, Windfury Weapon and Healing Wave current. After 30, Rockbiter ranks are for tanking only.
- **Elemental:** add Chain Lightning, Lava Burst (talent ranks 40/50/60) and keep Lightning Bolt current.
- **Resto/healer:** Lesser Healing Wave, Chain Heal, Healing Stream, Mana Tide and Riptide ranks (talent ranks are cheap).

**Money tips**
- Safe skips: all Resistance Totems, Far Sight, Sentry Totem, Windwall Totem, Stoneclaw Totem, Frostbrand Weapon.
- Higher Ancestral Spirit ranks only matter in groups.
- Call of the Ancestors / Call of the Spirits are convenience only; buy when gold is spare.
- Talent-gated ranks (Lava Burst, Riptide, Mana Tide) are cheap; buy them if you have the talent.
- Save for Apprentice Riding at 40; Ghost Wolf covers travel until then.
- Keep Ankhs for Reincarnation; Water Walking/Breathing need reagents.
