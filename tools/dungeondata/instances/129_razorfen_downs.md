## Forever changes noted by guides

- New reward Cultist's Chestguard (270054, leather chest) on An Unholy Alliance (6521)
- An Unholy Alliance rewards lowered rare->uncommon (Oct 8 beta)
- XP raised on four quests; escort and both Amnennar quests unchanged
- Most boss items restatted (e.g. Silky Spider Cape movement-impair reduction, Glutton's Cleaver bleed 70/7s, Coldrage Dagger slow, Bonefingers mana restore)
- Item-started quests not shareable since Oct 1 beta; Bring the End shareable, Bring the Light not
