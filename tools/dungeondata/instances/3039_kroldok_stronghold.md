## Forever changes noted by guides

- Entirely new Forever dungeon, launched Nov 4; Blizzard corrected level from 40-55 to 40-45
- Quest XP not split in groups
- Quest item drops party-shared since Nov 6 hotfix
- Mounts usable inside
- Krol'dok Battlegear set (item-set 2135): chest 275005 seen, other pieces unknown
- Counts toward Experienced Spelunker (62032)
