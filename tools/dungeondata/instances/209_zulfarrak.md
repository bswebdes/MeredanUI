- Level: 44-54 · Entrance: Tanaris · Faction: both
- Status: **live** - classic dungeon
- Sources: https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-dungeons-overview/, https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/dungeons/, https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-zulfarrak-guide/
