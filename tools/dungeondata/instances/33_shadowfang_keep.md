## Forever changes noted by guides

- Group cap 5 (page says Classic 10)
- Seal of Sylvanas: 6 AP instead of Strength
- Boss abilities much stronger (Arugal Void Bolt 1,399; Rethilgore Soul Drain 178 vs Classic 35)
- Rugged Spaulders blue, +2 all resists; Bloody Apron spell power; Commander's Crest/Baron's Scepter health regen; Fel Steed Saddlebags BoP
- Six new rare boss drops (~1.7-2%): Sorcerer Collar, Blade of Silverlaine, Worgenbane Talisman, Blindwatcher's Sight, Half-Eaten Boots, Worgpelt Leggings; plus Cell Keeper's Claws
- New quest rewards: Tattered Mittens 270030, Tanned Shoulderpads 270023, Bronzed Shoulderguards 270024
- Black Tome 276561 -> Tome of Dalaran 275989 at Ambermill Leyline Focus; summons secret boss in City of Dalaran (zone=16560)
- Blade of Silverlaine can be turned into Coldflame Saber item=276631 by Mages
