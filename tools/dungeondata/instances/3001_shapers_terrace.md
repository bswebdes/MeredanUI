# Shaper's Terrace (map 3001)

- Level: 58-60 · Entrance: Un'Goro Crater (northern hills) · Faction: both
- Status: **live** - new Forever dungeon, open since launch (Nov 4); not in Wowhead's dungeon journal yet; NPCs exist on Wowhead Forever without zone
- Sources: http://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24301455, https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-dungeons-overview/, https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-shapers-terrace-guide/, https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/shapers-terrace/
- Item/NPC/quest checks: Wowhead Forever tooltips (`https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=ID`), fetched 2026-11-10, rechecked 2026-11-16
- Keys / attunement: None announced. Un'Goro Power Crystals (11184/11185/11186/11188) are used at consoles inside.

## Research notes

- Warcrafttavern says there are likely more bosses than these four
- Pylon quests (related_zone_quests) are NOT tied to the dungeon by Blizzard per guide
- Unconfirmed raid portal behind a closed titan gate north of the dungeon
- Quests open at 55 (level 60 quests); quest reward item IDs 279901-279932 (conquestcapped)
- Console bosses spawn immediately when a console is fed - do not feed during a trash pull
- Exit: jump off the terrace ledge (teleports out)
- WH-F % for Shaper's Terrace items are from very few kills (e.g. Titanosaur 10 kills), treat as rough
