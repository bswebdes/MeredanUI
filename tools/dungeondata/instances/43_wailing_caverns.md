## Forever changes noted by guides

- All boss items blue, many with spell power/new effects
- New rare items (1-3%): Cloak of Hermitic Bliss, Slither Cord, Skum's Bucket, Snake Eye Kaleidoscope
- New blueprint drops: Journeyman Campfire (Serpentis), Field Guide (Anacondra)
- Cobrahn, Skum, Mutanus hit several times harder than Classic
- Worn Turtle Shell Shield now blue with bonus armor and mana regen
- Slime-encrusted Pads gain health/mana regen; Mutant Scale Breastplate usable from 18
- Firebelcher gained fire proc
- Embrace of the Viper set rebuilt
- New quest rewards: Hammerbone 270018, Heat Resistant Mitts 270008, Safety Boots 270009
