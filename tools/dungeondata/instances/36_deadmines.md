## Forever changes noted by guides

- Rhahk'Zor Slam ~330 dmg (Classic 64-86) + 3s stun; Smite Slam ~220 (Classic 50); several abilities ~4x harder
- Five bosses gained new items; most drops have new stats
- Rockslicer mines Copper/Tin/Silver/Iron without Mining
- Smelting Pants smelt 25% faster
- Staff of Westfall gains healing/spell dmg and 2% move speed
- Cookie's Stirring Rod +25% Cooking speed
- Defias Leather set rebuilt, mostly BoP, new set bonuses
- New quest chain: Destruction in Deadmines (92742-92753, 92819)
- New rings: First Mate Band 284715 (Smite) and partner Band of the Better Half 284716 (lvl 60 epic)
