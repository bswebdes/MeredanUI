# Blackrock Spire (map 229)

- Level: 55-60 · Entrance: Blackrock Mountain · Faction: both
- Status: **live** - classic dungeon (LBRS 55-60, UBRS 59-60; one map ID)
- Sources: https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-dungeons-overview/, https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/dungeons/, https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-lower-blackrock-spire-guide/, https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-upper-blackrock-spire-guide/
- Item/NPC/quest checks: Wowhead Forever tooltips (`https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=ID`), fetched 2026-11-10
- Keys / attunement: LBRS no key. UBRS needs Seal of Ascension (quests 4742/4743 from Vaelan npc=10296; needs Unadorned Seal item=12219; gemstones 12336/12335/12337; forged by Emberstrife npc=10321 -> Forged Seal of Ascension item=12324). One holder opens the door for the group. Onyxia attunement chains (Drakefire Amulet 6502 / Blood of the Black Dragon Champion 6602) run through UBRS (Blood of the Black Dragon Champion item=16663 from Drakkisath).
